Operational notes:
  - The bot is a singleton writer; the chart runs a one-replica StatefulSet so a replacement
    pod is never created until the old one has fully terminated. Never scale it, never run a
    second release against the same maker, and never force delete the pod or its Node
    (kubectl delete --force / --grace-period=0) — force deletion is Kubernetes' documented
    exception to StatefulSet at-most-one semantics. Fence a failed node before replacement.
  - Shutdown drains the in-flight cycle, then invalidates owned bootstrap and ladder groups
    serially, each wait bounded by the receipt timeout; the pod may legitimately take minutes
    to stop (for a chart-managed config the grace period budgets one receipt-bounded wait per serialized shutdown step, derived from the configured markets and groups).
  - The image is only re-pulled when the pod template changes: after a mutable tag such as
    `latest` moves, roll the pod with `kubectl rollout restart`, or set
    forceRestartOnUpgrade=true to roll on every `helm upgrade`.
